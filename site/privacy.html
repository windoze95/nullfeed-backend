Privacy.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

NullFeed is a client for your own self-hosted media server. The short version: your data lives on your server and your devices. We don't collect it, we don't see it, and there's nothing for us to sell, share, or lose.

What the app collects

Nothing. The NullFeed apps (iOS, Android, Apple TV, web) contain no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Where your data goes

  • The app connects only to the NullFeed server you configure — typically one running on your own hardware or hosting.
  • Profiles, watch history, playback progress, and your library live in that server's database, under your control.
  • A small amount of state (your server address, session, and offline downloads) is stored locally on your device so the app works.

What we receive

Nothing. The app never phones home to us. There are no NullFeed accounts, no cloud service, and no third-party data processors.

Push notifications

If your server enables push notifications, delivery uses the platform's push service (such as Apple Push Notification service). The payload comes from your server; we operate no notification infrastructure that reads your content.

Children's privacy

NullFeed collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, changes should be rare and boring.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repositories.
